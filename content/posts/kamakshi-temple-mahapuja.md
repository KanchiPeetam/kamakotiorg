A special mahapuja will be conducted at the ancient Kamakshi Amman Temple under the auspices of the Kanchi Kamakoti Peetam.

## Divine Occasion

The upcoming mahapuja at Sri Kamakshi Amman Temple marks an auspicious occasion for devotees to seek the blessings of the Divine Mother.

## Event Details

- **Date**: December 28, 2025
- **Time**: 6:00 AM onwards
- **Location**: Sri Kamakshi Amman Temple, Kanchipuram
- **Special rituals**: Abhishekam, Alankaram, Mahadeepam

## Significance

Sri Kamakshi Amman is the presiding deity of Kanchipuram, one of the seven sacred cities of India. This mahapuja is conducted with traditional Vedic procedures under the guidance of learned scholars.

## Participation

All devotees are welcome to participate in this sacred event. Special arrangements will be made for prasadam distribution.

## Temple Timings

The temple will remain open throughout the day for devotees to have darshan of the Goddess.

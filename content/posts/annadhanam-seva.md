The Mutt's daily annadhanam program serves thousands, embodying the principle that feeding the hungry is the highest form of service.

## A Sacred Tradition

Annadhanam (food donation) is considered one of the highest forms of dana (charity) in Hindu tradition. The Kanchi Kamakoti Peetam has been conducting this noble service for decades.

## Daily Operations

Every day, the Mutt provides:

- Fresh, nutritious meals to thousands
- Free food distribution to the needy
- Prasadam to all devotees
- Special arrangements during festivals

## Impact on Society

This selfless service has touched countless lives, ensuring that no one in the vicinity goes hungry.

## How to Contribute

Devotees can participate in this sacred service through donations and volunteer work.

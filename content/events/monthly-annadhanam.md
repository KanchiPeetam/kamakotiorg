Our monthly Annadhanam (food service) program provides nutritious meals to all who visit. This service is conducted on the first Sunday of every month.

Volunteers are welcome to help with:
- Food preparation
- Serving
- Distribution
- Cleanup

For volunteer registration, please contact the Mutt office.

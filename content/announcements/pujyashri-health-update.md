title: "His Holiness Pujyashri's Health Update"
date: 2025-12-22
summary: "We are pleased to inform devotees that His Holiness is in good health and continues his daily spiritual activities."
---

Following recent inquiries from devotees worldwide, we are happy to report that His Holiness Pujyashri Shankaracharya Swamiji is in good health.

His Holiness continues to:
- Perform daily poojas and meditation
- Grant darshan to devotees
- Provide spiritual guidance
- Bless ongoing activities of the Mutt

Devotees may visit for darshan during regular hours (6:00 AM - 12:00 PM and 4:00 PM - 8:00 PM).

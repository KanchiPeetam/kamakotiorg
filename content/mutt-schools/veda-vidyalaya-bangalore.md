Sri Veda Vidyalaya, established in 1995 in Bangalore, represents a unique blend of traditional wisdom and modern education. The school follows the ICSE curriculum while maintaining strong roots in Indian culture.

## Educational Philosophy

The school believes in nurturing the whole child - intellectually, emotionally, physically, and spiritually. Education is not just about academics but about character building and value formation.

## Infrastructure

- Spacious, well-ventilated classrooms
- Digital learning facilities
- Science and computer labs with modern equipment
- Meditation and yoga hall
- Well-stocked library
- Playground and sports facilities

## Special Programs

### Value Education
Daily moral science classes and character-building activities are integral to the curriculum.

### Arts and Culture
Students learn classical arts including Carnatic music, Bharatanatyam, and traditional crafts.

### Environmental Awareness
The school maintains an organic garden where students learn about sustainable living and environmental conservation.

## Parent Involvement

The school encourages active parent participation through regular meetings, workshops, and cultural programs.

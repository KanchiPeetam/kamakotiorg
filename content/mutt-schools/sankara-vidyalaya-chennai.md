Sri Sankara Vidyalaya, established in 1985, is one of the premier educational institutions run by Sri Kanchi Kamakoti Peetam. Located in the heart of Chennai, the school provides quality education from kindergarten through Class XII.

## Academic Excellence

The school follows the CBSE curriculum and maintains consistently high academic standards. Students are encouraged to develop critical thinking skills alongside mastering the core subjects.

## Facilities

- Well-equipped science and computer laboratories
- Extensive library with over 10,000 books
- Sports facilities including playground and indoor games
- Music and dance classrooms
- Transportation services

## Values and Culture

Education at Sri Sankara Vidyalaya goes beyond academics. Students are taught traditional values, Sanskrit, and participate in regular spiritual activities, creating well-rounded individuals.

## Admissions

Admissions are open throughout the year based on availability. Entrance tests are conducted for Classes VI onwards.

Sri Sankara Senior Secondary School in Kanchipuram is the flagship educational institution of the Mutt, established in 1978. The school offers both residential and day school options.

## Unique Features

### Sanskrit and Vedic Education
All students receive comprehensive training in Sanskrit language and basic Vedic concepts alongside their regular curriculum.

### Residential Facilities
The school provides excellent hostel facilities with separate accommodations for boys and girls, creating a disciplined learning environment.

## Academic Programs

- Classes from VI to XII
- CBSE curriculum with additional Sanskrit emphasis
- Regular workshops and seminars
- Career guidance and counseling

## Co-curricular Activities

Students participate in:
- Yoga and meditation sessions
- Classical music and dance
- Sports and athletics
- Debates and elocution competitions
- Community service programs
